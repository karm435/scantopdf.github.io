About Paperly

Paperly is the best "PDF maker app" that revolutionizes how people handle documents. Our app allows users to turn images into PDFs instantly, with advanced OCR technology, AI-powered chat features, and seamless document management.

Founded with the mission to simplify document workflows, Paperly has become the go-to solution for professionals, students, and anyone who needs to convert images to professional PDF documents quickly and efficiently.

Key Facts

  • App Name: Paperly
  • Category: Productivity / Document Management
  • Platforms: iOS, iPadOS, macOS
  • Launch Date: 2024
  • Core Feature: Image-to-PDF conversion with OCR
  • Unique Selling Point: AI-powered chat with PDF content

Company Story

Paperly was born from a simple yet powerful idea: making document management effortless and accessible to everyone. We recognized that in today's digital world, people still struggle with paper documents, receipts, and handwritten notes.

Our team of developers and designers came together with a shared vision to create an app that would bridge the gap between physical and digital documents. What started as a simple image-to-PDF converter has evolved into a comprehensive document management solution that millions of users rely on every day.

Key Features

  • Smart Capture: Select photos from gallery or scan documents using camera
  • Advanced OCR: Extract text from images with high accuracy
  • AI Chat: Ask questions about your PDF content
  • Smart Search: Find specific words with automatic highlighting
  • Quality Control: Adjust PDF quality settings as needed
  • Offline Functionality: Work without internet connection

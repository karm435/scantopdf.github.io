What is Paperly?

Paperly is the best "PDF maker app" that allows you to turn images into PDFs instantly. You can select photos from your gallery or scan paper documents using your camera, combine them into a single PDF file, and keep everything organized.

What features does Paperly offer?

Paperly offers several powerful features including:

  • Smart image capture and scanning
  • Advanced OCR text extraction
  • AI-powered chat with PDF content
  • Smart search functionality
  • Instant PDF creation and sharing
  • Quality settings for PDF output

How does the OCR feature work?

Paperly uses advanced Optical Character Recognition (OCR) technology to extract text from images. Once you load your images, the app automatically processes them to recognize and extract text. You can then use the smart search feature to find specific words, which will be highlighted and automatically scroll to the search results.

Can I chat with my PDF documents?

Yes! Paperly includes an AI-powered "Chat with PDF" feature. You can ask questions about your PDF content, and the AI will reply based on the information extracted from your loaded images and documents. This makes it easy to quickly find information without manually searching through your documents.

What file formats does Paperly support?

Paperly supports all common image formats including JPEG, PNG, HEIC, and more. You can import images from your photo library or capture new ones using your device's camera. The app then converts these images into high-quality PDF documents.

Is Paperly available for both iPhone and iPad?

Yes! Paperly is designed to work seamlessly on both iPhone and iPad. The app automatically adapts to your device's screen size and provides an optimized experience for both iOS and iPadOS. You can also use it on macOS for a complete cross-platform experience.

Is Paperly free to use?

Paperly offers a free version with basic features. For advanced features like unlimited PDF creation, enhanced OCR, and priority support, you can upgrade to our premium subscription. Check the App Store for current pricing and subscription options.

Can I use Paperly offline?

Yes! Paperly works offline for most features. You can capture images, create PDFs, and manage your documents without an internet connection. Some advanced features like AI chat may require an internet connection for optimal performance.

How do I share my PDFs?

Paperly makes sharing PDFs incredibly easy. Once you've created your PDF, you can share it via email, AirDrop, messaging apps, or save it to your device. The app also supports cloud storage services like iCloud, Dropbox, and Google Drive for seamless file management.

Is my data secure with Paperly?

Absolutely! Paperly prioritizes your privacy and data security. Your documents are processed locally on your device, and we don't store or access your personal files. The app works offline, ensuring your sensitive documents remain private and secure.
